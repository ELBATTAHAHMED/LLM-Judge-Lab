\section{Coverage and exclusions}

The ledger intentionally gives different denominators for different RQs. A research question that requires a decisive AB/BA comparison cannot use the denominator from a repetition experiment; a strict consensus result cannot use a DUAL\_SWAP retained denominator. Using a common denominator simply for visual uniformity would be scientifically incorrect. Complete-case analysis prioritizes a transparent eligibility rule over an artificial 100\% completion claim.

\section{Historical comparison boundary}

Historical analysis runs remain available for audit and comparison, but they are not the authoritative values in this report. The source-corrected final lineage retained the direction of the historical RQ effects while correcting the underlying source text and final denominators.
